Nutrition guide

Protein: beans, lentils, tofu, yogurt and eggs if you eat them.

Fiber: whole grains, fruit, vegetables and legumes.

Iron: lentils, beans and fortified cereals. Pair with vitamin C foods such as citrus or peppers.

Calcium and B12: dairy or fortified plant alternatives. Discuss individual supplement needs with a clinician.

Added sugar differs from naturally occurring sugar in fruit and milk. All foods can fit into a varied diet.

For individual energy and nutrient needs, consult a registered dietitian or healthcare professional.
